1. Product Mental Model & Core Purpose

The application is an autonomous AI traffic router. Instead of sending all queries to slow, expensive frontier models ($1.25/1M) or fragile cheap models ($0.05/1M), the Disjoint LinUCB Contextual Bandit reads prompt features in under 0.1ms and switches queries to the optimal tier among 6 calibrated models.
